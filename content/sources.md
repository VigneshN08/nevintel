NEVINTEL pulls a fixed list of publisher feeds. It does not crawl, it does not
scrape article pages, and it does not follow links beyond the feed itself.

Two things are worth stating plainly. First, **a source's presence here is not
an endorsement and not a partnership** — these are public RSS feeds, read the
way any reader's feed reader reads them. Second, **the health table below is
not decorative.** Publishers block automated clients, feeds move, and
scheduled builds get dropped under load. If a feed has been failing for a
week, this page says so.

Publishers: if you would prefer NEVINTEL show only your headline and link, or
drop your feed entirely, say so and it is done. There is no appeals process to
navigate and no form to fill in.
